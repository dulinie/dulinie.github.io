My story

Three chapters, so far

Chapter one · Sri Lanka

Building a career, 2007–2022

I grew up in Colombo, Sri Lanka, where a Fundamentals of IT course sparked my interest in technology. I went on to study with NIBM Sri Lanka and the British Computer Society (UK).

I joined John Keells IT as an Associate QA Analyst on airline reservation systems and grew into a QA Lead, working on 35 projects for local and international clients. Along the way, I helped roll out Azure DevOps, mentored junior QAs, and ran internal quality audits (CMM Level 3, ISO 9001:2015).

I also worked closely with clients and users, from requirement discussions and system demos to UAT at client sites, training real users, and solving production issues and client queries.

Chapter two · California

Family, adventure and a new home, 2022

Moving to California was an exciting chapter for me and my family, filled with new challenges, new landscapes and new adventures.

I took a deliberate career break to be a full-time mom to my two young children. After years of nonstop study and work, I truly enjoyed being home with them, watching them grow and making beautiful memories together. I am truly grateful for this time.

Chapter three · Today

From traditional QA to AI-assisted QA

After 15 years of traditional QA, I'm excited to bring that experience into modern, AI-assisted test automation. Since 2026, I've been refreshing my skills by building real projects:

  • Selenium framework: Java, TestNG and Maven, with Page Object Model, parallel runs and rich reports.
  • CI/CD: tests run automatically with GitHub Actions, Azure Pipelines and Jenkins.
  • Playwright + TypeScript: an AI-assisted project for a banking demo app, reviewed and verified by me.
  • Test management: Azure DevOps Boards and Test Plans.
  • AI as my QA partner: Claude, Gemini and Copilot, with every output validated by me.

My goal: to become a hands-on QA Engineer in the era of AI-driven test automation.
